Motown Museum

This old Detroit home belies the greatness that was born here: the Motown Sound. But one step inside Hitsville, USA, the original house where Berry Gordy lived and recorded the Motown hits, and you'll catch the beat and spirit of this booming music era. Photos, costumes, artwork, music and other memorabilia take you back to simpler times.

Opening Times

  • Tuesday - Closed
  • Wednesday - 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Thursday - 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Friday - 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Saturday - 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Sunday - 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Entry-price

  • Adults (18-61) - $20
  • Children (5-17), students, seniors (62+) and veterans - $17
  • Children (4 or under) - Free

Public Transport

Bus Routes: The museum is accessible by bus routes 16, 29, 42, and 18. The closest bus stop is Cass & Columbia, which serves as a transfer point for these routes.

Fox Theatre

Detroit's crown jewel, the Fox Theatre, has played host to some of the biggest names in show business and holds its ground as one of the top-grossing theaters of its size in the nation.

  • Sunday - Closed

Entry-price

  • $35-$149

Public Transport

Bus Services: Take the DDot bus from Woodward & Gratiot to Woodward & Columbia, which operates every 20 minutes. The journey takes approximately 2 minutes.

Greektown

The ouzo flows and the souvlaki sizzles on Monroe Street, the center of an ethnic section of Detroit dominated by Greek restaurants and businesses.

Public Transport

SMART Bus Route 261: This bus runs from Detroit to downtown, and you can transfer to the QLine streetcar at Greektown (Monroe Street) for a fare of $2 for a 3-hour pass.

Location on Google Maps

Comerica Park

Home of the Detroit Tigers, this is no ordinary ballpark. Combination theme park, ballpark, and baseball museum, it features huge statues of tigers, a Ferris wheel, carousel (with tigers, of course) and a fountain that celebrates each home run with colored lights and music.

Entry-price

  • $36-$409

Public Transport

QLINE is a 6.6-mile circulating streetcar loop serving 12 locations on Woodward Ave. from Downtown Detroit through Midtown, New Center and the North End. Hours of operation are 6AM - 11PM Monday-Thursday, 6AM - 12AM, Friday & Saturday, 8AM - 8PM Sundays.
