When to visit?

Best times to come

The best time to visit Detroit is from late spring through early fall (May through September) when temperatures are comfortable and social activities are plentiful.

Where to stay?

Best places to stay

All of the hotels in this list are in downtown Detroit, within walking distance or easily accessible by public transportation to the best restaurants and attractions. You can stay in cheaper accommodations, but you may have to stay outside of the downtown, and then drive in (it costs roughly $15 per day to park your car in Detroit).

  • LUXURY: MGM Grand
  • UPSCALE: Aloft at the David Whitney
  • MID-RANGE: Hotel Indigo Detroit Downtown
  • BUDGET: Comfort Inn Downtown

Transportation

Best transport

For travel within downtown and Midtown, the People Mover and QLine are efficient and easy to use. Use Google Maps or the Transit app for real-time public transit instructions and schedules. Carry exact change for cash payments on People Mover, or use a QLine mobile app.
