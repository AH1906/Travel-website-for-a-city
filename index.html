Detroit, located on the Detroit River across from Windsor, Ontario, is the most populous city in Michigan and the largest U.S. city on the Canada–United States border, with a population of 639,111 as of the 2020 census. The broader Metro Detroit area has over 4.4 million residents, making it the 14th-largest metropolitan area in the U.S. and the second-largest in the Midwest. Founded in 1701 by French explorer Antoine de la Mothe Cadillac as Fort Pontchartrain du Détroit, the city grew into a major industrial hub in the Great Lakes region.
